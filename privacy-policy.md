# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 2026**

## Introduction

This privacy policy helps you understand how information is handled within the **Lock Eye** mobile application. Lock Eye is built as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the terms outlined in this policy. Lock Eye is designed with a strong commitment to user privacy, prioritizing local data processing, private on-device storage, and minimal data collection.

## Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, Lock Eye uses certain device features to provide its core security and intruder-detection functionality. Here is exactly how data is handled:

* **Captured Images:** Lock Eye can capture an image when a wrong password, PIN, or pattern unlock attempt occurs, depending on the features and permissions enabled by the user.
    * Captured images are processed and stored **locally on your own device**.
    * Images are stored in a private folder associated with the Lock Eye application.
    * We do not upload, copy, or transfer these captured images to our own external servers or cloud storage.
    * These images are intended only to help the device owner identify failed unlock attempts.

* **Failed Unlock Attempt Information:** Lock Eye may process information necessary to detect a failed unlock attempt and trigger the application's security features.
    * This information is processed locally on the device.
    * Lock Eye does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

* **Alarm Feature:** Lock Eye can optionally play an alarm or alert when a wrong unlock attempt occurs, according to the settings selected by the user.
    * The alarm functionality operates locally on the device.
    * No personal information is required to use this feature.

* **Personal Identifiable Information:** Lock Eye does not require user registration, profiles, or accounts. We do not request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, gender, physical address, or phone contacts.

## Cookies

Lock Eye does not directly use website cookies because it is a mobile Android application.

However, third-party services integrated into the application, such as advertising or analytics services, may use identifiers, SDK technologies, or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

## Device Information

Lock Eye may access information necessary for the application to provide its functionality on your Android device.

This may include technical information required for detecting failed unlock attempts, operating the alarm feature, capturing images, and maintaining the application's functionality.

Any information required for these core features is processed locally on the device unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy.

Lock Eye does not use this information to create a personal profile or require users to identify themselves.

## Camera Permission

Lock Eye requires camera access to provide its intruder-detection functionality.

When enabled and supported by the device, the camera may be used to capture an image following a failed unlock attempt.

Captured images are stored locally in the application's private storage area on the device. Lock Eye does not upload these images to its own servers or cloud storage.

Camera access is used only for the functionality provided by Lock Eye.

## Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals when necessary to facilitate our Service, provide advertising, or support technical functionality.

Third-party service providers may process limited device-level information according to their own privacy policies and the permissions granted by the user.

Lock Eye does not provide captured intruder images or personal user information to third parties for purposes unrelated to providing the application's functionality.

## Security

We value your trust in using our application.

Captured images generated by Lock Eye are stored locally in the application's private storage area on your Android device. Lock Eye does not maintain an external server containing your captured intruder images.

Because data stored on the user's device can potentially be accessed if the device itself is compromised, rooted, or otherwise improperly secured, users should maintain appropriate security protections on their Android device.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Deletion

Lock Eye does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to our servers.

Lock Eye does not maintain a server-side database containing user accounts or captured intruder images.

Because captured images and related application data are stored locally on the user's device, users can remove them through the application's available deletion features or by clearing the application's data or uninstalling Lock Eye.

No separate account deletion request is required because Lock Eye does not maintain user accounts or server-side personal data.

## Children’s Privacy

The application does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
